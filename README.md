# TrikeIt!

_Affordable, hyper-local micro-deliveries powered by neighborhood tricycle drivers._

**Built for CSSPRINT 2026 by Team Ones**

## Overview

Standard delivery platforms charge high base fares, making hyper-local and low-value deliveries economically unviable for neighborhood Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

TrikeIt! solves this by connecting local businesses with neighborhood tricycle drivers. Our platform unlocks affordable micro-deliveries for merchants while boosting driver income during off-peak hours.

## Key Features

### Smart Matching & Delivery

- **Route-Based Barangay Dispatching:** Utilizes mapped routes and assigned TODA (Tricycle Operators and Drivers' Association) zones across barangays nationwide to automatically match nearby MSME orders with the appropriate local driver.
- **Pasabay Delivery (Dual Passenger & Parcel):** Enables drivers to accept micro-deliveries along their route while carrying regular passengers. This maximizes driver income per trip without disrupting standard passenger service.
- **Multi-Stop Drop-Offs:** Automatically batches multiple neighborhood orders heading in the same direction, reducing delivery costs for stores and optimizing travel time for drivers.

### Payment & Merchant Tools

- **Flexible Payment System:** Supports both Cash-on-Delivery (COD) and popular cashless payment methods (GCash, Maya) to ensure accessible transactions for customers and instant payouts for drivers.
- **Simple MSME Dashboard:** Provides small business owners with a clean overview of delivery history, completed orders, and accumulated costs, enabling them to track expenses and identify peak delivery times easily.
